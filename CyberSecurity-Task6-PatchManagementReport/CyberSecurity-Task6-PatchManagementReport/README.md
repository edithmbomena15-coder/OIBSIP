# Task 6: Research Report — The Importance of Patch Management

This report explains what patch management is, why unpatched systems are
one of the largest attack surfaces in cybersecurity, and how organisations
can build an effective patching strategy.

## Contents
- [patch_management_report.md](patch_management_report.md)

## What the report covers
- Definition of patch management and its role in the vulnerability lifecycle
- How vulnerabilities are discovered, catalogued (CVE) and scored (CVSS)
- Real-world breaches caused by unpatched systems: WannaCry (2017) and
  Equifax (2017)
- Consequences of not patching, with statistics
- The five-phase patch management lifecycle: Discovery, Assessment,
  Testing, Deployment and Verification
- A prioritised seven-step patch management checklist
- Common challenges and how to overcome them
